Web Teleoperation for Lab Robots

Reserve a robot, open your browser and drive it from anywhere — Pepper, Unitree Go2 and more.

Year
2023 – 2025
My role
Architect · full-stack developer
Stack
Node.jsExpressMongoDBWebRTCROSNAOqiPython

The problem

International students in remote programs needed hands-on time with real robots located in Osaka.

What I built

  1. Platform with user accounts, a booking system per robot, usage dashboards and an admin panel.
  2. Per-robot control interfaces with live video, movement and speech for a SoftBank Pepper (NAOqi) and a Unitree Go2 quadruped.

Outcome

Built for remote robotics programs with international students.
