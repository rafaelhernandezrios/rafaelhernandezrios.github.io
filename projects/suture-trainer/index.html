SutureTrainer: Neuroadaptive Laparoscopy Simulator

A Unity simulator for laparoscopic suturing that reads the trainee’s cognitive load and adapts the session.

Year
2026
My role
Researcher · Unity & hardware integration
Stack
UnityC#OpenXR3D Systems TouchLSLAURA EEG

The problem

Surgical skills training is cognitively demanding; a simulator that ignores the trainee’s mental state can overload novices or under-challenge experts.

What I built

  1. Unity training scenes for laparoscopic suturing, operated with VR controllers and with a 3D Systems Touch haptic robot.
  2. Live AURA EEG over LSL to estimate cognitive load during each activity.
  3. Experimental protocol comparing an adaptive group against a non-adaptive group.

Outcome
