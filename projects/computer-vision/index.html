Applied Computer Vision

YOLOv8 pest and strawberry detection, GPT-Vision inventory counting and OCR meter reading.

Year
2023 – 2025
My role
Developer
Stack
PythonYOLOv8PyTorchOpenCVFlaskOpenAI VisionTesseract

The problem

Agriculture and industry partners asked for quick, low-cost prototypes to automate visual inspection.

What I built

  1. YOLOv8 models with a Flask web app for pest detection, and a strawberry detector for video.
  2. Can and bottle counting with GPT Vision as a zero-training alternative.
  3. Water-meter reading: serial image capture plus OCR.

Outcome

Working prototypes for demos and partner proposals.
