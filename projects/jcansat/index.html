JCansat v2

Redesign of an educational CanSat: component audit, architecture decision records, pinout, BOM and KiCad migration.

Year
2026
My role
Hardware lead
Stack
KiCadESP32SensorsADRs

The problem

The first JCansat worked in workshops but was hard to manufacture and maintain.

What I built

  1. Audit of every component and eight ADRs documenting the architecture decisions.
  2. Pinout and interconnection reference, BOM and a step-by-step guide to KiCad.

Outcome

A documented base for the next hardware revision used in CanSat programs.
