VR Relaxation with EEG — Expo Osaka 2025

Visitors relax in 360° environments while their EEG computes a live relaxation index — then the system replays the scene that relaxed them most.

Year
2025 – 2026
My role
GUI design · relaxation-detection algorithm · exhibition engineering
Stack
A-FrameElectronPythonMNELSLUnityMeta Quest

The problem

Make EEG understandable for the general public in a few minutes, at a busy international exhibition, with zero setup failures.

What I built

  1. A-Frame participant app over HTTPS that works offline on a LAN, a Python LSL → WebSocket bridge and an Electron operator monitor.
  2. Exhibition mode: a timed playlist of five environments plus a replay of the “winner” with the highest average relaxation index.
  3. Later ported to a Unity demo for Meta Quest with a bilingual EN/JP operator console and a brain heatmap.

Outcome

Shown at Expo Osaka 2025 and adapted for 5G lab demos.
