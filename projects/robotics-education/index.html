Interactive Robotics & IoT Teaching

Hands-on material for my classes and workshops: interactive kinematics, IoT pipelines, serial/TCP/LSL practices.

Year
2024 – 2026
My role
Instructor · author
Stack
ReactViteESP32MQTTPythonArduinoWeb Serial API

The problem

Students learn robotics faster when they can play with the math and build an end-to-end system in a single session.

What I built

  1. Interactive web lesson for forward/inverse kinematics of a 2R planar robot, with singularities and activities.
  2. 3-hour remote IoT workshop: ESP32 + DHT22 (Wokwi) → MQTT → anomaly detection in Colab → actuation.
  3. Workshop series on serial communication with Arduino, TCP sockets, LSL, desktop GUIs and Web Serial dashboards.

Outcome

Taught to university students in person in Osaka and online.
